01

How to use this with an AI model

Paste the brand book, then this guide, then the request. The brand book carries the rules and the token values; this guide carries the shapes. Neither is a suggestion.

Copy tokens verbatim

Take the §12 block whole. Never adjust a value, never add a grey, never introduce a hue that is not there.

Build bilingual first

Logical properties, t() on every string, both directions from the first commit. Retrofitting RTL is a rewrite.

Name your gaps

Where the system is silent, pick a sensible default, say you chose it, and do not present it as part of the brand.

The three failures
Why it fails
Brand green as text

Colour-only status
Roughly one man in twelve cannot separate the amber from the green. Always an icon and a word.
02

The logo

A portrait mark and a signature wordmark, locked together. The green stroke through the hair is part of the mark, not an accent that can be recoloured. Five approved variants — nothing outside this set.

The approved set
Primary

Portrait plus signature, horizontal lockup. The default everywhere it fits.

Primary + tagline

"An Ecosystem Builder" on a green bar. First impressions only — decks, covers, signage. Not in product UI.

Wordmark

Signature alone, for narrow spaces and anywhere the portrait would drop below its minimum size.

Symbol

Portrait alone, for avatars, favicons and app icons. Only where the name is already established.

Negative

White portrait and wordmark for dark grounds. The green may stay on the hair stroke or drop out entirely.

Negative, on grey
One-colour, on saturated grounds
Clear space and size
  • Clear space on all four sides equals the cap height of "Altorabi". Nothing enters it.
  • Primary lockup: 120px minimum on screen, 30mm in print.
  • Symbol: 32px minimum. Below that, the beard and brow detail fills in.
  • Below 120px, switch to the wordmark rather than shrinking the portrait.
Placement
  • The logo never mirrors in Arabic layouts. It sits at the start of the reading direction.
  • On photographs, use the negative variant over a flat dark area — never over busy detail.
  • One logo per surface. It does not repeat as a pattern or watermark.
Misuse
  • Do not stretch, rotate, outline or add a shadow.
  • Do not recolour the portrait or the signature to a hue outside the palette.
  • Do not reset the wordmark in another typeface — it is drawn lettering, not live text.
  • Do not separate the portrait from the signature to build a new lockup.
  • Do not place the full-colour logo on a mid-tone where the dark ink falls below 4.5:1.

Typography

STC is the single bilingual family, covering Latin and Arabic in one face at Light 300, Regular 400 and Bold 700. IBM Plex Mono stays for figures and code, where STC has no tabular set. Two families, no third. Do not substitute Inter, Poppins or Space Grotesk.

Bold 700 · headings, labels, buttons

There is no 500 or 600. Where a component in this guide reads font-weight:600, the browser renders STC Bold — that is intended. Never let a renderer synthesise the missing weights.

Always
  • Western numerals 0 1 2 3 in both languages.
  • tabular-nums wherever digits align in a column.
  • Arabic at +1px and +0.2 line-height.
  • Arabic face applied by direction, not by class.
Never
  • letter-spacing on Arabic, in any amount.
  • text-transform: uppercase on Arabic — it silently does nothing.
  • A third typeface, faux bold, faux italic, or synthesised 500 / 600 weights.
  • Arabic-Indic digits ٠١٢٣ in interfaces.
Working with STC

Adopted at the client's direction, superseding §03 of the brand book. Three weights ship — 300 / 400 / 700 — so there is no 500 or 600: emphasis goes straight from Regular to Bold, and any UI that wanted a semibold uses Bold. Confirm the licence covers web embedding and product use, and confirm Latin accent coverage before shipping a European locale. Figures are not tabular, so columns of numbers stay in IBM Plex Mono.

07

Illustration and icons

One drawing language, taken from the mark: even strokes, round caps, geometric construction, a single flat green accent on an otherwise neutral line drawing. No gradients, no soft shadows, no isometric scenes, no faces, no 3D.

Construction
  • Drawn on a 24px grid, scaled in multiples.
  • Stroke 1.75px at icon size, 2.5px at spot size.
  • Round caps and round joins, matching the mark.
  • Rectangles take a 3–4px corner radius, never sharp.
Colour in drawings
  • Line in --ink-2 or --rule-strong, never pure black.
  • One accent per drawing: --green-wash fill with a --green-ink line.
  • Brand green appears only as a small solid fill, never as a hairline.
  • Status colours in a drawing only when the drawing is about status.
Never
  • Stock vector people, blobs, glassmorphism, 3D renders.
  • Gradient fills of any kind.
  • Decoration in an empty state that carries no meaning.
  • An illustration doing work a single number should do.

Spot illustrations · 64px

Each carries one idea and no more than five shapes. Use them in empty states, onboarding steps and section headers.

Icons · 20–24px

Lucide, unmodified, at 1.75px stroke. Do not mix icon sets and do not redraw them. An icon never appears alone as a control unless it carries an aria-label and a tooltip.

Treatment
  • Natural colour. No duotone, no green wash over the image.

  • Illustration for structures, flows and states.

10

Arabic and RTL

Arabic is a first-class language here, not a translation layer. Build both directions from the first commit — retrofitting RTL is a rewrite, every time.

Logical properties only
margin-inline-start
padding-inline-end
border-inline-start
inset-inline-start
text-align: start

Never margin-left. One physical property and the Arabic layout breaks silently.

Mirrors
  • Back, forward, next, previous arrows.
  • Progress and stepper direction.
  • Sidebar, drawer and breadcrumb order.
  • Chart reading direction, not the chart's own axis maths.
Does not mirror
  • The logo and the mark.
  • Clocks, play and pause, media transport.
  • Numbers, currency figures, phone numbers, code.
  • Checkmarks, warning glyphs, brand imagery.
Copy is written, not translated

Arabic runs 20–30% longer than English. Buttons must flex, never fix their width. Write the Arabic natively in Modern Standard Arabic — formal enough for a board paper, plain enough to read once. Machine translation shows immediately and costs credibility.

11

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, not the goal. Every ratio in §02 is measured, not estimated.

Requirement
Rule
Text contrast
4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for text at 24px+ or 19px+ bold.
Non-text contrast
3:1 for input borders, focus rings, icons carrying meaning.
Hit area
44×44px minimum on every interactive target, including icon-only buttons.
Focus visible
2px ring, 2px offset, --green-ink on light and #7FD094 on dark. Never remove the outline.
Never colour alone
Status always carries an icon and a word alongside the colour.
Reduced motion
Honour prefers-reduced-motion. Transitions 150–250ms, ease-out, and never longer than 400ms.
Opacity on text
Forbidden. Step down to --ink-2 or --ink-3, which keep their measured ratios.
